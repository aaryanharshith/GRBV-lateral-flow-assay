Key results

How this works

Virions in extract N = titer × tissue mass × ηext. Concentration C = N / (NA × Vbuffer). Bound complexes Nb = min(N, Nsites × factive × θ × fkin × ftransport × fhook). Signal S = iunit × Nb. LOD = titer at which Nb reaches the visual threshold (bisection).

Assumes all extract reaches the strip, a single-site Langmuir occupancy, and a saturated detection step on the captured particle. Sources: see References.

Parameters

Curves

1. Binding curves (Langmuir)

How this works

θ = C / (C + Kd), Langmuir 1918 [1]. Curves scale your Kd by ×0.1, ×1 and ×10.

2. Concentration to intensity

How this works

I = Ibg + iunit × Nb(titer). Kinetic factor fkin = 1 − exp(−tinc/τ), τ = 1/(konC + koff). Transport factor ftransport = 1/Da for Da > 1, with Da = konC δ²/D and D from Stokes–Einstein scaling [2]. Hook factor fhook = 1/(1 + C/Ch) [A, empirical].

3. True / false positive rates vs threshold

How this works

Uninfected: I ~ N(Ibg, σn), σn = √(σreader² + σmatrix²). FPR = Q(z). Infected: titer lognormal around the titer slider (ln-SD [U]). Se = Σ wi Q(z − Si/σn). Q is the Gaussian tail, computed with an erf approximation (Abramowitz & Stegun 7.1.26) [3].

4. Predictive values vs prevalence

How this works

Bayes' theorem [4]: PPV = Se·p / (Se·p + FPR·(1−p)); NPV = (1−FPR)(1−p) / ((1−FPR)(1−p) + (1−Se)·p). Evaluated at the current threshold z.
